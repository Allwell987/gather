Gather

A faith-based community app connecting believers for Bible study, devotionals, prayer, church groups, events, and fellowship throughout the week.

About Gather & Application Purpose

Gather is the official community and fellowship mobile application operated by The Wealth Reality Church, located in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. Our core purpose is to foster spiritual growth, community engagement, and seamless connection among church members and leadership.

Through Gather, members can:

  • Read and Study the Bible: Access scripture readings, daily devotionals, and save personal verse highlights.
  • Engage in Community Prayer: Submit prayer requests, read and encourage others, and participate in interactive prayer chains.
  • Participate in Church Groups: Connect with small groups, departments, and fellowship units via group discussions and updates.
  • Discover and RSVP Events: Stay informed about upcoming church services, youth programs, and special gatherings.
  • Support Ministry & Giving: Safely contribute tithes, offerings, and project donations via secure payment integrations (Paystack).
  • Receive Notifications: Get timely devotion reminders and church announcements via push notifications.

Google APIs & Terms of Service Compliance

Gather integrates select Google APIs solely for core app functionality and user convenience, adhering strictly to the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0): Used optionally to authenticate users securely and streamline account creation using basic profile info (name, email, profile picture) provided by Google. Gather does not request or access sensitive scopes such as Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or Contacts.
  • YouTube Data API v3: Used exclusively to retrieve and display public video content from official church YouTube channels within the app interface.

Strict Prohibition of AI-Generated NCII:
Gather does not use Google APIs, Google user data, or any integrated AI services to create, request, process, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII). Gather features no AI image generation capabilities whatsoever, and strictly prohibits the generation or dissemination of non-consensual intimate imagery.

Data Collection & Privacy Commitment

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information. We collect only data necessary for account authentication, profile management, and community interactions (such as email, name, uploaded profile photos, and prayer/group messages). Payment card details are processed directly by Paystack and are never stored on our servers.

Read our full Privacy Policy for complete details on data collection, usage, third-party service providers (Supabase, Sentry, Paystack, Google), data retention, and how to request account deletion.
